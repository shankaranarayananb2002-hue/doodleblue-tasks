Tech Fest 2026

Technical

Annual flagship technology festival, hackathons, and innovation symposium.

About The Event

Tech Fest 2026 is the university's annual gathering of student programmers, makers, and innovators. Over two action-packed days, students engage in hackathons, robotics challenges, code debugging, and tech talks from industry specialists.

Highlights

  • 24-Hour Hackathon with cash awards and startup mentorship.
  • Robo-Wars and Autonomous Drone obstacle competition.
  • Guest Keynotes from leading tech executives.
  • Certificate of participation for all attendees.

Rules & Eligibility

Open to all registered undergraduate and postgraduate students. Teams must contain 2 to 4 members. Please ensure you bring your valid college ID card to the venue for verification.
