About Campus Events

Campus Events is a student-centric platform that organises and promotes a wide range of events including technical, sports and academic activities. Our goal is to encourage participation, creativity and collaboration among students.

Our Mission

Engage Students

Provide opportunities to explore, learn and showcase talents across multidisciplinary spheres.

Build Community

Connect students across departments, batches and shared creative and technological interests.

Create Opportunities

Organise meaningful events, career networking, and real-world experiences throughout the academic year.

Our Event Types

Technical

Hackathons, tech talks and innovation events.

Cultural

Music, dance, art and literary events.

Sports

Intra and inter-college competitions.

Workshops

Skill development and industry sessions.
